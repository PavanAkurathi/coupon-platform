# HOLD / Chenchulu trust rules

Founder decision, October 15, 2026. These are hard requirements for every feature, commercial agreement and release. Growth or revenue cannot override them. They define our conduct and make no claim about a competitor's conduct.

1. **Never take someone else's credit.** No cookie access, affiliate-tag replacement, hidden tracking redirects or background attribution capture. Preserve prior creator, publisher and merchant attribution. If protection cannot be established, do not monetize the transaction. Coupon-code attribution must be reviewed too; unknown behavior blocks automatic application.
2. **Rank for the shopper.** Commission data stays outside ranking. Never hide an eligible better offer or working code because it pays us less, pays nothing or conflicts with a paid placement. Apply the same evidence and eligibility standards to paying and nonpaying stores. Disclose commercial relationships.
3. **Prove the savings.** Measure improvement over the starting checkout, including existing discounts. Preserve a better existing code. Never present advertised discounts, estimates or unverified results as savings we delivered. Missing coverage means unknown, not “best price.” Never fabricate coupon evidence, urgency, earnings or success rates.
4. **Respect businesses and creators.** Use public or expressly authorized offers within their restrictions. Never distribute private, personal or creator-specific codes as general coupons; invent merchant agreements; bypass access restrictions; or charge for credit we did not earn. Do not promise another party's commission is protected unless that protection is established.
5. **Make user choice meaningful.** No silent affiliate activation or misleading controls. Any future monetized action needs a clear explanation of the benefit and our compensation before the shopper deliberately chooses it. No forced purchases, hidden fees or obstructive opt-outs. Existing no-order and no-payment-field rules remain in force.
6. **Collect only what the feature needs.** No sale of browsing or shopping histories, covert cross-site profiling or unnecessary collection of checkout information. Explain data use, permissions and retention. Provide straightforward ways to disable optional features and delete saved data.
7. **Keep the exact $1 rule.** Retain $1 only when additional shopper savings are confirmed, earned merchant commission is at least $1, prior attribution is protected and the order has not already been settled by us. Otherwise retain zero. Never deduct this fee from shopper savings; handle reversals honestly. This document enables no revenue feature.
8. **Enforce before release.** A feature or commercial deal that cannot meet these requirements does not ship. Code changes need evidence and regression checks for the boundaries they affect. If a violation is discovered, disable the affected behavior, investigate and correct it; communicate material user or partner impact honestly.

This is binding product policy, not a claim that every planned safeguard is already implemented or that the product is launch-ready. Existing ownership, authorization and merchant safety gates still apply.
