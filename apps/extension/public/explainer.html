Coupons, with evidence.

Chenchulu helps you inspect offers and review what a checkout actually shows. You decide what to buy and place the order yourself.

What a coupon means

On Amazon, we show supported visible offers and can focus their controls. A selected coupon is not a confirmed discount. Your eligibility and final total must be checked on Amazon.

An Amazon order-summary reading shows what Amazon reports. It does not mean Chenchulu found that discount, that an order was placed, or that a commission was earned.

Suggested checkout codes need approval for public use, reviewed neutral attribution and recent shopper confirmations. Availability varies by store. Shopify automatic code testing is not available in this version.

Savings above your starting total

Where testing is available, it starts only after your click. Additional savings means the improvement over the total before the test, including any discount already there. A code's full discount and the final order total are shown separately.

If the checkout changes or a result cannot be confirmed, review your cart. We do not turn an uncertain result into a savings claim.

Prices and earnings

Price comparison is a separate, optional tool. It checks product identity, condition and quantity, and uses known shipping when making a comparison. Prices are before tax; confirm the store's final total.

Commission data does not affect ranking. This version adds no affiliate links, earns no commission from its store links, and offers no cash back or rewards. We do not guarantee another publisher's commission.

What stays in your control

  • No orders are placed and no payment fields are read.
  • We do not read shopping cookies or replace affiliate tags.
  • Saved items stay in this browser until you remove them. Backups let you move your list deliberately.
  • Price alerts use the store-page permission you choose. Saving an item does not create scheduled monitoring.
  • We use only the page information needed for the requested check. Coupon verification reports, when enabled, exclude payment and address details.
